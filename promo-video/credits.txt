3D textures and models
- Poly Haven (polyhaven.com) could NOT be used: api.polyhaven.com and dl.polyhaven.org were blocked by the
  network policy of the render environment. Instead every PBR texture set (albedo / normal / roughness) was
  generated procedurally by scripts/gen_textures.py (seeded FFT noise, original work, no third-party content):
  wood floor planks, cream plaster, dark varnished wood, painted steel, brass, paper, book-spine atlas,
  oil-painting (lighthouse seascape) and rug.
- All models (bookshelf + books, desk, banker's lamp, gilded painting frame and picture light, Edison pendant,
  chest, 4-dial combination padlock, key, steel exit door, keypad, alarm beacon, wall clock) were built from
  Three.js geometry (beveled RoundedBoxGeometry etc.) in src/room.js.
- Ambient reflections: Three.js RoomEnvironment (procedural, MIT).

Software
- three.js r169 (MIT) — https://threejs.org
- GSAP 3.12.5 (GreenSock standard "no charge" license) — https://gsap.com
- Playwright 1.56 (Apache-2.0) with Chromium/SwiftShader for frame capture
- numpy / scipy (BSD) for audio synthesis, Pillow for textures, ffmpeg for encoding

Music and sound
- 100% synthesised in code by scripts/audio.py (120 BPM, seeded RNG). No samples, no copyrighted music.
